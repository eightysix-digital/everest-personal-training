MISSION & VISION

What we are here to do

Two sentences we hold ourselves to, and are willing to be measured against.

Mission

We build everyday capability through structured movement, and we measure it honestly, so what works can be proven, repeated and scaled.

Capability is strength, movement and confidence: the things that decide how someone lives, works and copes. We coach it in person in Canterbury and online in several countries, and we record what changes.

Vision

Movement recognised and funded as frontline prevention — for the body and the mind.

Not a private benefit for the few who can already afford a coach, but part of how communities and health systems keep people well. That is a long road, and it is the one we are on.

If you are one person

You are the point, not a step towards something bigger. Everything we have learned about programming, measurement and what makes training stick started with individuals and comes straight back to them. The ambition below changes nothing about the coaching you get.

If you are an organisation

You get structured delivery, consistent measurement and reporting you can put in front of a board or a funder. We would rather show you a smaller number we can defend than a large one we cannot.

The Everest journey

From a single commitment to a growing human performance organisation.

1

The problem

Many people want to improve their health but lack the structure, confidence, support or access to make it stick. Good intentions run into busy lives, intimidating gyms and one-size-fits-all advice.

2

The origin

Everest began with a simple commitment: make training more personalised, practical and sustainable. Coaching that meets people where they are, in the environments they actually train in.

3

The evolution

Over time, that coaching expanded into app-based digital training, youth development, performance, organisational wellbeing and community initiatives, while keeping the same focus on real people and real outcomes.

4

What we believe

Capability is foundational. Strength, movement and confidence underpin how people live, work and show up, so building them is some of the highest-value work anyone can invest in.

5

Where we are going

The model is deliberately built to travel. Coaching, the app, the programme structure and the measurement all work the same way whether someone is in Rolleston or on another continent, and we already coach clients well beyond New Zealand. The aim is to keep proving it locally, then take what holds up wherever it is needed.

What we believe

Capability changes what's possible.

Physical capability supports confidence, independence, productivity and long-term health. Building it is one of the highest-leverage things a person, a workplace or a community can do.
