OUR IMPACT

The impact we work towards

Outcomes that go well beyond weight on a scale.

For the people we coach, progress shows up as confidence in everyday life, strength that transfers well beyond the gym, and habits that actually last. For the schools, workplaces and partners we work alongside, it shows up as participation, engagement and capability we can measure and report.

How we handle evidence

Credibility matters more than big numbers. We hold ourselves to a clear evidence hierarchy, strongest evidence first, and never publish unverified or inflated figures.

1

Verified quantitative outcomes

Participation, adherence, retention, strength and movement change, program completion and validated survey results.

2

Case studies

A defined starting point, intervention, duration and measured outcome.

3

Testimonials

Shared with full permission and appropriate context.

4

Partner feedback

Feedback from partners and repeat engagements.

5

Program methodology

Program descriptions and delivery methodology.

6

Community & media

Community images, media coverage and milestone records.

Every public statistic is recorded in an evidence register noting its source, date range and calculation method before it appears on this site.
