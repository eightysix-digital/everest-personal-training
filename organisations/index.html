Workplace wellness across Christchurch and Canterbury

Local delivery for employers and organisations throughout the region.

Everest delivers corporate wellness, workplace fitness and preventative human performance programs to employers, councils, emergency services and health partners across Christchurch and Canterbury. We design programs around your people, sites and shift patterns, deliver on site or online, and report on the outcomes that matter.

From a single-site pilot to a multi-location rollout, our programs are governed, measurable and built to scale, so you can demonstrate real impact to your leadership, board or funders.
