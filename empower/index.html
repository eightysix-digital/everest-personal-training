What is EMPOWER

A structured youth development programme

EMPOWER teaches children important life skills through the lens of movement, health and wellbeing. Each session combines guided physical activity with age-appropriate learning to help children better understand their bodies, their minds and how to take care of both.

Move well

Physical literacy, coordination, strength foundations and safe movement habits.

Feel strong

Confidence, emotional awareness, self-belief and resilience through challenge.

Thrive together

Teamwork, communication, leadership and positive social connection.

Why it matters

Building healthy habits before problems begin.

Many children are growing up with less movement, more screen time and increasing pressure on their physical and emotional wellbeing. EMPOWER supports children early by giving them the tools, confidence and habits to engage with movement positively for life.

Exercise should not only be used after a problem appears. EMPOWER supports a preventative approach by helping children develop capability, confidence and wellbeing habits early.

How sessions work

Designed for real-world delivery

EMPOWER sessions are structured, age-appropriate and designed to work in schools, after-school care environments, community programmes and sports settings.

1

2

3

4

An age-appropriate health or wellbeing learning point for the day.

5

Reflection & close

A reflective, confidence-building close to finish each session.

The partnership

Launching in partnership with Busy Bumbles

EMPOWER is proud to launch its pilot programme in partnership with Busy Bumbles during Term 4, 2026. Busy Bumbles has shown strong leadership in supporting children beyond traditional after-school care, allowing EMPOWER to be tested in a real community environment with children, families and staff.

From 21 October to 25 November 2026, the pilot will involve 75 children across a structured 6-week programme, helping shape future delivery across schools, after-school care providers and community settings.

Measurable by design

A programme designed for measurable impact

EMPOWER is not designed as a one-off activity. It is being developed as a measurable youth development programme. During pilot delivery, we aim to track:

  • Attendance
  • Participation
  • Completion rates
  • Child enjoyment
  • Movement confidence
  • Physical literacy
  • Health and wellbeing knowledge
  • Parent feedback
  • Staff feedback
  • Safety and delivery quality
