Youth fitness across Christchurch and Canterbury

Building confident, capable young people throughout the region.

EMPOWER delivers youth fitness and athletic development across Christchurch and Canterbury, helping young people build movement skills, strength and confidence in a safe, supportive environment. We run term programs and holiday camps, and partner with schools and clubs to deliver age-appropriate coaching at their own venues.

Every session is led by qualified coaches with safeguarding built in, so families, schools and community organisations can have confidence in how programs are delivered.

EMPOWER questions, answered

What ages is EMPOWER for?

EMPOWER is designed for young people, with age-appropriate coaching tailored to each group. Get in touch and we will confirm the right fit for your child or group.

Where are EMPOWER sessions held?

Sessions run in Christchurch and across Canterbury, including options for schools and community organisations at their own venues.

Are your coaches qualified and safeguarded?

Yes. EMPOWER is delivered by qualified coaches with youth-protection practices and appropriate boundaries built into every session.

Do you work with schools and clubs?

Yes. We offer term programs, holiday camps and tailored options for schools, clubs and community organisations across Canterbury.
