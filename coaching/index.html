Do you offer personal training in Christchurch?

Yes. We offer in-person personal training and coaching across Christchurch, plus online coaching for clients anywhere in the world.

Can I do online coaching if I am outside Christchurch?

Absolutely. Our online coaching delivers your programming, accountability and feedback through an app, and we run 1-on-1 consultations by video call, so you can train from anywhere in the world. We currently coach clients in New Zealand, South Africa and Canada.

What is the difference between an app program and personalised coaching?

App programs give you structured training to follow yourself. Personalised coaching is built specifically around you, with regular check-ins, technique feedback and a coach who adjusts the plan as you progress.

Do you coach beginners as well as athletes?

Yes. We coach everyone from first-time gym users building confidence through to experienced athletes chasing performance goals.
